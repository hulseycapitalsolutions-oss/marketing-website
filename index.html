How do soft credit pulls protect my business profile?

The Operational Advantage: When evaluating heavy equipment loans or working capital lines up to $10,000,000, traditional hard credit inquiries can temporarily depress your corporate and personal credit scores. Hulsey Capital Solutions utilizes initial soft credit pulls for pre-qualification and structuring. This allows our institutional lenders to review your financial profile without generating hard bureau inquiries, protecting your borrowing power until you formally approve final loan documents.

  • Zero Bureau Impact: Initial pre-qualification generates no hard credit footprints.
  • Fast Assessment: Instantaneous evaluation of cash flow and credit tier eligibility.
What are the maximum age restrictions for used heavy machinery?

The Operational Advantage: Unlike standard commercial banks that refuse financing on machinery older than 5 years, our specialized institutional partners recognize the residual value and durability of well-maintained industrial iron. We finance used heavy excavators, bulldozers, wheel loaders, and Class 8 semi-trucks with flexible age parameters, provided the asset passes a certified multi-point mechanical inspection.

  • Extended Asset Age: Financing available for machinery up to 10 to 12 years old on select commercial tiers.
  • Inspection Backing: Certified mechanical inspection reports ensure reliable job-site performance from day one.
Can I bundle working capital and maintenance reserves into my equipment loan?

The Operational Advantage: Absolutely. Acquiring heavy machinery or expanding a transport fleet requires immediate auxiliary capital for initial maintenance, commercial insurance down payments, fuel overhead, and operator payroll. Our structured loan packages allow you to bundle working capital directly into your primary equipment financing tranche, preserving your existing cash reserves.

  • Single Streamlined Payment: Combine asset acquisition and working capital into one predictable monthly installment.
  • Immediate Liquidity: Secure cash reserves for unexpected job-site mobilization and parts inventory.
